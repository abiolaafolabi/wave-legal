Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. The short version

Wave keeps what you write on your device. There is no account, no Wave server, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect your data, so we have nothing to sell or share.

2. Who this applies to

Anyone using Wave, which is built in the UK by Abiola Afolabi, with research and input from Beatrice Widmark. You must be at least 16 to use it.

3. What is stored, and where

The following is saved only on your device:

  • The logs, notes and journal entries you write
  • Where you placed each log on the wave (Order or Chaos)
  • Photos and voice notes you choose to attach to a log
  • Your name, if you gave one, and what you told us brought you to Wave
  • Your saved strategies, collections and any tools you create
  • Your settings, such as light or dark mode and cycle frequency

None of this is sent to us. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

4. Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone — only to record a voice note when you tap record
  • Photos — only to attach an image to a log when you choose one

You can say no and keep using Wave, and you can change these any time in your phone's settings.

5. Backups

Your phone's own backup service (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include Wave's data, depending on your device settings. That is between you and Apple or Google, and you control it in your phone's settings.

6. Third parties

We do not use advertising or analytics services. The one outside service involved is Expo, which delivers app updates: when Wave starts, it may ask Expo's servers whether a newer version is available. That request carries the standard technical details any internet request does (such as your IP address, app version and device type), but never anything you have written. See expo.dev/privacy for how Expo handles that.

7. Sharing

Wave does not share your data with anyone. Friends and sharing features are not available yet; before they are, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what would be shared and with whom, and it will be your choice.

8. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual logs in the app, and uninstalling Wave removes everything it stored on your device. Copies in a phone backup are removed according to your backup settings.

9. Your rights under UK GDPR

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on your behalf — you already have full control of it in the app. You still have the right to:

  • Ask us what data we hold about you (we expect the answer is none)
  • Object to how data is handled, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)

Questions or requests: joinwaveapp@gmail.com.

10. Children

Wave is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from under 16s. Since Wave collects nothing, there is no data from them to remove, but if you have concerns please contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

If Wave changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, adding accounts or syncing — we will update this policy first and tell you in the app before anything changes.

12. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email joinwaveapp@gmail.com.
