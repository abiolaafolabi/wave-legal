Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. The short version

Wave keeps what you write on your device. There is no account, no Wave server, no advertising, no analytics and no purchases in this beta. We do not collect what you write in the app, so we have nothing to sell or share. The only personal details we hold are the email addresses of people who join the waitlist on our website, and, if you are invited, the email address we used to invite you to the beta (see "The waitlist" and "Getting the beta through TestFlight" below).

2. Who this applies to

Anyone using Wave, which is built in the UK by Abiola Afolabi, with research and input from Beatrice Widmark. You must be at least 16 to use it.

3. What is stored, and where

The following is saved only on your device:

  • The logs, notes and journal entries you write
  • Where you placed each log on the wave (Order or Chaos)
  • Photos (chosen from your library or taken with the camera) and voice notes you attach to a log
  • Your name, if you gave one, and what you told us brought you to Wave
  • Your saved strategies, collections and any tools you create
  • Your settings, such as light or dark mode and cycle frequency
  • If you use the Apple Watch app, the logs you make on the watch, which are kept on the watch and copied between your watch and your phone (see "Apple Watch" below)

None of this is sent to us. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, and photos and voice notes are copied into the app's own storage when you save a log.

4. Permissions we ask for

  • Notifications — only if you choose a daily reminder. It is scheduled by your phone itself and never contains what you wrote
  • Microphone — only to record a voice note when you tap record
  • Photos — only to attach an image to a log when you choose one
  • Camera — only to take a photo for a log when you choose to

You can say no and keep using Wave, and you can change these any time in your phone's settings.

5. Apple Watch

If you use the Wave app on your Apple Watch, your logs, the skills you tried and your saved skills are copied between the watch and the phone directly, using the connection Apple provides between the two. Nothing goes through a Wave server. On the watch you can add a line to a log by dictating it. Dictation is done by Apple's own text input on your watch, not by Wave. Apple may process the audio of what you say, and the text it produces, to turn it into words, as set out in Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). Wave never receives or stores the audio. Only the text you choose to save is kept, on your watch. You can type or scribble instead of dictating, or skip the line altogether.

6. Backups

Your phone's own backup service (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include Wave's data, depending on your device settings. That is between you and Apple or Google, and you control it in your phone's settings.

7. Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules a notification itself, at the time you chose. It is a local notification: nothing is sent from a server, and it never contains anything you wrote. You can switch it off in Wave (Profile, then Notifications) or in your phone's settings.

8. Third parties

We do not use advertising or analytics services, and the app has no purchases in this beta. The outside services involved are Expo, which delivers app updates; Apple, which runs TestFlight and the App Store, and Apple's dictation on the watch; and (only for the website waitlist, not the app) MailerLite, which holds the waitlist. For Expo, when Wave starts, it may ask Expo's servers whether a newer version is available. That request carries the standard technical details any internet request does (such as your IP address, app version and device type), plus a random ID for the installed app that Expo uses to count update installs. It is not linked to your name or anything you have written, and never includes your logs. See expo.dev/privacy for how Expo handles that.

9. Getting the beta through TestFlight

The beta is delivered through Apple's TestFlight. Apple runs TestFlight and handles your Apple ID. If we invited you by email, we hold the email address we typed into App Store Connect to send the invitation. Depending on your TestFlight settings, Apple may tell us basic information such as which build you installed, how often you opened it and any crash reports, and anything you choose to send as feedback (for example a screenshot or a comment). Apple, not Wave, decides what TestFlight shares; see apple.com/legal/privacy. Wave itself does not send us your logs, notes, photos, voice notes or moods.

10. The waitlist

If you join the Wave waitlist on our website, we collect your email address and whether you chose iPhone or Android. We use them only to confirm your sign-up, to invite you to the Wave beta when a place is available, and to email you about getting access to Wave. They are kept with our email provider, MailerLite, which acts on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email we send, and you can ask us to delete your details by writing to joinwaveapp@gmail.com. This is separate from the app, which still stores what you write only on your phone.

11. Sharing

Wave does not share your data with anyone. Friends and sharing features are not available yet; before they are, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what would be shared and with whom, and it will be your choice.

12. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual logs in the app, and uninstalling Wave removes everything it stored on your device. Copies in a phone backup are removed according to your backup settings.

13. Your rights under UK GDPR

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on your behalf — you already have full control of it in the app. You still have the right to:

  • Ask us what data we hold about you (we expect the answer is none)
  • Object to how data is handled, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)

Questions or requests: joinwaveapp@gmail.com.

14. Children

Wave is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from under 16s. Since Wave collects nothing, there is no data from them to remove, but if you have concerns please contact us.

15. Changes to this policy

If Wave changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, adding accounts, analytics or purchases — we will update this policy first and tell you in the app before anything changes. Paid plans are planned for a future release; when they arrive, this policy and the terms will be updated first.

16. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email joinwaveapp@gmail.com.
